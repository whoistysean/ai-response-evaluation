Explain photosynthesis in exactly three sentences for a 12-year-old. Make sure your explanation is scientifically accurate and easy to understand.
AI Response : 
Photosynthesis is how plants make their own food: they take in sunlight through their leaves, water through their roots, and carbon dioxide from the air. Inside tiny parts of their cells called chloroplasts, a green substance named chlorophyll captures the sunlight's energy and uses it to turn the water and carbon dioxide into sugar, which the plant uses for fuel and growth. As a bonus, the plant releases oxygen as a by-product, which is the very oxygen we breathe.
