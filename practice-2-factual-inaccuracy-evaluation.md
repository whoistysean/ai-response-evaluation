AI Response Evaluation — Practice 2
Project Objective
The objective of this exercise is to demonstrate my ability to identify factual inaccuracies in AI-generated content, assess scientific correctness, and explain evaluation decisions.
Evaluation Prompt
Explain photosynthesis in two sentences.
 AI-Generated Response
“Photosynthesis is the process by which plants breathe in oxygen and use it to create food. Plants absorb sunlight through their roots and use it to produce sugar.”
Evaluation Verdict
Verdict: INCORRECT
The AI-generated response contains multiple scientific inaccuracies.
Identified Errors
Error 1: Incorrect use of oxygen
The response claims that plants use oxygen to create food during photosynthesis. However, plants primarily use carbon dioxide, water and light energy to produce sugar.
Error 2: Incorrect sunlight absorption
The response claims that plants absorb sunlight through their roots. In reality, chlorophyll in chloroplasts captures light energy, mainly in the leaves.
Correct Scientific Explanation
Photosynthesis is the process by which plants use light energy, water and carbon dioxide to produce glucose (sugar). Oxygen is released as a by-product of this process.
Evaluator Reasoning
I classified the response as incorrect because it misrepresents the materials and processes involved in photosynthesis. It incorrectly identifies oxygen as an input and roots as the location where sunlight is absorbed. These errors make the explanation scientifically unreliable.
Skills Demonstrated
Identifying factual inaccuracies.
Scientific information verification.
Critical thinking and analytical reasoning.
Evaluating AI-generated content.
Explaining and justifying evaluation decisions.
Final Assessment
The exercise demonstrates the importance of checking AI-generated information rather than accepting responses simply because they sound convincing. An effective AI evaluator must identify factual errors, understand why they are incorrect, and provide evidence-based explanations.
